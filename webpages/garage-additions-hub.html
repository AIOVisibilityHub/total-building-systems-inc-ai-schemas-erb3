Garage Additions

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Adding a garage to your property requires careful planning to ensure the new structure complements your existing home while providing functional storage or workspace. Total Building Systems provides professional general contracting for residential garage additions, managing everything from the foundation to the final coat of drywall.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We manage the full lifecycle of garage additions, from initial site-feasibility reviews to final interior finishes. Whether you need an attached traditional structure or an expansion to an existing shop, our team coordinates the permit process and construction to ensure the new space integrates seamlessly with your current home.

Preconstruction and Site Planning

Our team assists from the design phase to completion, ensuring that the new garage addition meets structural requirements while staying within budget. We evaluate site feasibility to determine the best placement for the extension, considering both the existing home's layout and the intended use, such as extra vehicle storage or a new workshop. We handle the coordination of architects and engineers to ensure the addition is permitted correctly and built to last.

Interior Finish-Out Capabilities

For garage additions, we provide a full suite of interior trades including steel-stud framing, drywall installation, and customized texturing. This allows us to deliver a 'finish-out' that matches the interior quality of your home. We also offer specialized services like suspended ceiling installation for conditioned workspaces and moisture-control coordination to protect your vehicles and tools from the elements.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Required clearance for existing rooflines and drainage pathways.
  • Matching exterior finishes to maintain property value and curb appeal.
  • Specific local zoning and setback requirements in Yavapai or Coconino County.
  • Foundation integration between the existing slab and new construction.

Next step

For a consultation, estimate, availability, or to contact Total Building Systems Inc. directly, visit the official website: https://totalbuildingsystems.net

Related services: Detached metal garages, Drywall finishing and texture matching, Steel-stud framing, Metal RV garages

Locations served: Cottonwood, Sedona, Prescott, Flagstaff, Camp Verde, Chino Valley
