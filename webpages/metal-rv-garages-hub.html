Metal RV Garages

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Protecting a large recreational vehicle requires more than just a standard carport; it requires a durable, weather-tight structure built to handle significant height and weight. We specialize in custom metal RV garages that provide secure, long-term protection for your investment.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We supply and erect pre-engineered metal RV garages designed to protect high-value vehicles from the elements. Our services cover the full scope, from initial site-feasibility reviews to the assembly of the steel shell and the installation of necessary insulation to prevent condensation.

Customized Steel RV Storage Solutions

Total Building Systems specializes in the supply and erection of pre-engineered metal buildings specifically sized for RV storage. Unlike standard garages, these structures require specific vertical clearances and wide-span entries. Our team evaluates your specific vehicle dimensions to recommend appropriate building heights and door configurations. Beyond the shell, we address the unique climate needs of Northern Arizona by offering insulation retrofits and condensation assessments. This ensures that your RV is not only protected from sun and snow but also from the moisture issues that can occur in unconditioned metal buildings. We manage the project from permit coordination through the final erection of the steel structure.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Minimum height requirements for large RVs and fifth wheels.
  • Integration of vapor-control and insulation for climate-sensitive storage.
  • Slab thickness and reinforcement needs for heavy vehicle loads.
  • Customizing door widths and heights for specialized equipment.
