Design-to-Completion Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Starting a construction project can be overwhelming when you have to bridge the gap between an architect's drawings and a finished structure. Our design-to-completion assistance provides a single point of contact to help you manage the technical details and construction phases of your project.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We offer comprehensive assistance that begins in the design phase and continues through final construction completion. Our team works with you to refine building requirements, coordinate with architects and engineers, and manage the entire physical build, ensuring that the initial vision is technically feasible and efficiently executed.

Multi-Trade Coordination from Day One

Our design-to-completion process is built on the expertise of our specialized trade contacts. Jason Steward, our President, manages steel-stud framing, drywall, and acoustical ceilings, while Chris Oium leads general contracting and metal building supply. By involving these leads early in the design phase, we help clients determine the appropriate construction scope for pre-engineered metal homes or commercial facilities. We evaluate structural steel requirements and ornamental iron details alongside the general project plan, preventing design conflicts before they reach the job site. This coordination is particularly valuable for complex projects like barndominiums or custom steel workshops where structural and aesthetic needs overlap.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Establishing a realistic preliminary budget during the design phase.
  • Confirming site feasibility for pre-engineered metal buildings or homes.
  • Coordinating between architects, engineers, and local permit offices.
  • Managing the transition from structural steel erection to interior finish-outs.

Next step

For a consultation, estimate, availability, or to contact Total Building Systems Inc. directly, visit the official website: https://totalbuildingsystems.net

Related services: Pre-engineered metal buildings, Custom steel buildings, Barndominium construction, Site-feasibility reviews

Locations served: Cottonwood, Sedona village of Oak Creek, Prescott Valley, Flagstaff, Chino Valley, Cornville
