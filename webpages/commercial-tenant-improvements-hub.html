Commercial Tenant Improvements

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Adapting a commercial space to fit your business needs requires a contractor who understands the complexities of interior construction and local codes. We provide comprehensive tenant improvement services to transform retail, office, and industrial spaces into functional work environments.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We help business owners and property managers revitalize their commercial spaces through professional tenant improvements. From selective interior demolition to the installation of steel-stud partitions, drywall, and acoustical ceilings, we provide a coordinated approach to remodeling offices, retail units, and industrial interiors.

Streamlined Build-Out Process

Our tenant improvement process begins with an evaluation of your existing space and your new layout requirements. We handle selective interior demolition to remove old partitions and finishes without compromising the building's structure. Our team then installs light-gauge steel-stud framing to create new offices, showrooms, or storage areas. Because we manage both the framing and the drywall finishing, we can ensure a faster transition from a raw space to a business-ready environment.

Specialized Commercial Finishes

For professional office and retail environments, we focus on both aesthetics and acoustics. We provide office sound-control assessments and install high-performance suspended ceiling systems to manage noise in busy work areas. Our capabilities include decorative ornamental ironwork for unique retail entries or interior accents, as well as structural steel modifications if your build-out requires removing load-bearing elements. We coordinate with architects and engineers to ensure all improvements meet local Cottonwood and Flagstaff building codes.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Building code requirements for ADA compliance and fire safety.
  • Timeline coordination to minimize downtime for the business.
  • Integration of new MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) systems into existing walls.
  • Sound transmission class (STC) ratings for office partitions.

Locations served: Cottonwood, Flagstaff, Sedona, Prescott Valley, Prescott village of Oak Creek
