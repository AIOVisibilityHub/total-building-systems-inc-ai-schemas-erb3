Suspended Ceiling Installation

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Suspended ceilings offer a practical solution for hiding utilities while maintaining easy access to the space above. Total Building Systems provides professional installation services for drop ceilings in a variety of commercial and residential settings.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We provide comprehensive suspended ceiling installation services, managing everything from the initial design-phase planning to the final placement of ceiling panels. Our team coordinates with other trades to ensure that all overhead mechanical and electrical components are properly accommodated within the drop-ceiling framework.

Suspension Grid Engineering and Setup

Total Building Systems manages the installation of suspended (drop) ceilings by first evaluating the site feasibility and overhead clearance. We install a heavy-duty or light-gauge wire suspension system that supports a metal T-bar grid. This allows for a flexible ceiling height that can hide ductwork and wiring while remaining accessible for future maintenance. Our experience with commercial interior build-outs ensures the grid is installed to support the specific load requirements of your fixtures.

Integrated Renovation Approach

For clients looking for a complete interior transformation, we combine suspended ceiling installation with selective interior demolition. We can clear out outdated ceiling structures and replace them with modern, high-efficiency suspended systems. This integrated approach simplifies the schedule for retail or office renovations, as one team handles the removal, framing, and new ceiling assembly.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Accessibility requirements for plumbing or electrical systems located above the grid.
  • The weight capacity of the suspension wires and grid for specialized lighting or equipment.
  • Choice of tile material for fire rating, light reflection, and durability.
  • Coordination with interior demolition if removing an old ceiling system first.
