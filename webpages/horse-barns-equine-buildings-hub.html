Horse Barns and Equine Buildings

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Protecting your horses requires a structure that is both resilient and well-ventilated. Total Building Systems specializes in the construction of metal horse barns and equine buildings, utilizing high-quality steel and custom ironwork to create a safe environment for your livestock.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We design and build durable metal equine structures, ranging from simple horse stalls to large-scale barns and arenas. By combining our metal building erection expertise with custom ornamental ironwork, we create safe, functional spaces tailored to the needs of both the owner and the animals.

Integrated Metal and Iron Construction

Our equine buildings utilize pre-engineered metal shells for maximum durability and low maintenance. We go beyond the shell by providing custom ornamental ironwork for stalls and gates, ensuring that the interior of the barn is as robust as the exterior. This integrated approach allows us to deliver a complete equine facility that handles the specific physical demands of housing large animals.

Planning for Equine Facilities

For larger equine facilities, we manage the site-feasibility and permit coordination required for agricultural or residential zoning. We evaluate the need for specialized interior demolition if a site is being repurposed, and we provide steel-stud framing for interior rooms like tack areas, offices, or feed rooms. Our design-to-completion assistance ensures that drainage, insulation, and layout are planned before the first piece of steel is placed.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Proper ventilation and airflow patterns for animal health.
  • Durability of interior partitions and stall structures against wear and tear.
  • Placement of wash racks and tack rooms relative to plumbing and drainage.
  • Safety features in ornamental ironwork to prevent injury to livestock.
