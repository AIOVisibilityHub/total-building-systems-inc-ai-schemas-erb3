Suspended Ceiling Repair and Replacement

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Damaged or outdated ceilings can negatively impact the look and safety of your space. Total Building Systems provides efficient suspended ceiling repair and replacement to address everything from minor tile damage to complete grid system failures.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

Total Building Systems offers dedicated repair services for damaged or sagging suspended ceilings. Whether you are dealing with water-stained tiles, bent grid components, or need a full system replacement, our team provides the specialized trade support to restore your ceiling's appearance and function.

Grid Restoration and Tile Matching

When a ceiling system is compromised by leaks or mechanical work, we perform targeted repairs. Our service includes removing damaged acoustical tiles, assessing the metal grid for level and stability, and replacing components as needed. We specialize in matching existing textures and styles to ensure the repaired area blends seamlessly with the rest of the ceiling. If the system is aged or severely damaged, we provide options for a complete replacement that meets modern building codes.

Post-Utility Repair Coordination

Our repair services are frequently utilized following plumbing or electrical work that requires access through the ceiling. We work as a subcontractor to quickly close up and repair suspended ceilings once the primary utility work is finished. This specialized focus ensures that the final interior finish remains professional and consistent across the entire facility.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Identifying the source of any water damage or stains before replacing tiles.
  • Matching the texture and color of existing tiles with current manufacturer products.
  • Checking the structural integrity of the suspension wires and wall angles.
  • Evaluating if a full grid replacement is more cost-effective than multiple spot repairs.
