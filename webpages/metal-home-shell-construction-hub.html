Metal Home Shell Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Building a new residence starts with a durable, weather-tight envelope that can withstand the elements. We specialize in metal-home shell construction, providing the structural steel foundation and exterior skin needed to get your project dried-in quickly.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We provide a streamlined path to a dried-in residence by supplying and erecting pre-engineered metal home shells. Our team handles the structural assembly, ensuring the steel frame and exterior panels are professionally installed to create a weather-tight envelope, allowing you to focus on the interior finishing at your own pace.

The Shell Erection Process

Our shell construction process focuses on speed and structural integrity. As both a supplier and erector of pre-engineered systems, we manage the delivery of steel components and the precise assembly of the primary frame, secondary members, and metal siding/roofing. This 'dry-in' phase provides a secure, durable structure that is ready for windows, doors, and interior partitions. By utilizing steel-stud framing for any exterior-integrated walls, we maintain a consistent, rot-resistant structural system throughout the home's perimeter.

Fit and Site Planning

A metal home shell is ideal for owners who want a high-performance building envelope but may wish to manage their own interior finishes or delay the finish-out phase. It suits those looking for the fire resistance and longevity of steel in Arizona's climate. However, because these are pre-engineered, major structural changes are difficult once the shell is fabricated; we evaluate your architectural plans during the design phase to ensure all structural loads and openings are accounted for before the steel arrives on-site.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Local building permit requirements for steel structures in Arizona.
  • Placement and sizing of door and window openings in the pre-engineered frame.
  • Site readiness including pad preparation and utility stub-outs.
  • Coordination between the shell erection and future interior trades.
