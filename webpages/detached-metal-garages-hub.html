Detached Metal Garages

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Adding a detached metal garage is an efficient way to protect your vehicles and increase property value. We provide turnkey and shell-only options for steel garages, RV storage, and custom additions designed to withstand the Arizona climate.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We specialize in the supply and erection of detached metal garages, RV storage buildings, and garage additions. Our team handles everything from structural steel and metal building kits to the interior drywall and electrical-ready framing, providing a secure and weather-tight environment for your vehicles.

Specialized vehicle storage solutions

Our experience includes a wide range of detached structures, from standard two-car garages to oversized RV garages and storage buildings. We handle the technical aspects of pre-engineered metal building supply and erection, ensuring the structure is properly anchored and sealed. For residential properties, we can coordinate the project from the design phase to ensure the new garage integrates with your site's layout and meets local building codes.

Interior finishing for garages

Beyond the exterior shell, we provide interior construction services that are often required for high-end garages. This includes steel-stud framing for interior rooms, drywall repair or installation, and acoustical ceiling work. If your garage will house valuable classic cars or sensitive equipment, we offer insulation retrofits and condensation-control services to maintain a stable environment.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • What are the height requirements for your tallest vehicle or RV?
  • Do you plan to include a workshop or storage loft within the garage?
  • Is a concrete slab already in place or does it need to be coordinated?
  • Does the exterior need to match an existing residential home?
