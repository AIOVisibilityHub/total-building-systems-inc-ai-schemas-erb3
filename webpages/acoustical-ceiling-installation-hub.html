Acoustical Ceiling Installation

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Enhancing a room's acoustics and concealing overhead utilities requires a precisely installed ceiling system. Total Building Systems offers expert acoustical ceiling installation to help property owners and contractors achieve high-quality sound management and a professional finish.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

Total Building Systems provides professional acoustical ceiling installation for both new construction and interior renovation projects. As a specialized subcontractor, we coordinate the installation of the suspension grid and acoustic tiles to ensure a level, secure finish that meets your project's specific sound-control and aesthetic requirements.

Professional Grid and Tile Installation Process

Our team handles the complete setup of acoustical ceiling systems, beginning with the layout and installation of the metal suspension grid. We ensure the grid is perfectly level and properly secured to the structure above. Once the framework is in place, we fit the selected acoustic tiles, ensuring clean cuts around perimeters, lighting fixtures, and air vents. This process is often performed alongside our steel-stud framing and drywall services to provide a seamless interior finish-out for offices or retail environments.

Coordinated Interior Trade Services

Total Building Systems serves as a single point of contact for multiple interior trades. Because we specialize in structural steel, steel-stud framing, and drywall in addition to acoustical ceilings, we can effectively manage the interfaces between these systems. This coordination is particularly useful for commercial tenant improvements where wall partitions must meet the ceiling grid precisely to maintain sound privacy and structural integrity.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Current ceiling height and overhead clearance requirements for the grid system.
  • Integration with existing lighting, HVAC diffusers, and fire sprinkler systems.
  • Acoustic performance needs, such as sound absorption (NRC) and sound blocking (CAC).
  • Moisture and humidity levels in the space that may affect tile longevity.
