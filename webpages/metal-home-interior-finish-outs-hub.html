Metal Home Interior Finish-Outs

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Once your steel shell is standing, the focus shifts to creating a livable, insulated, and beautiful interior. We provide specialized finish-out services designed specifically for the unique structural requirements of metal homes and barndominiums.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We transform a raw steel shell into a comfortable, functional living space through comprehensive interior finish-out services. Our team handles the specialized requirements of metal buildings, including steel-stud framing, high-performance insulation, drywall, and acoustical ceilings to complete your home's interior.

Interior Trade Coordination

Finishing the interior of a metal building requires specific trade expertise, particularly in moisture and sound control. We coordinate the installation of metal-building insulation to prevent condensation and improve thermal performance. Our crews then utilize light-gauge steel-stud framing for interior partitions, which offers superior straightness and durability compared to wood. We follow this with professional drywall installation, including specialized texture matching like orange-peel or knockdown to ensure the interior feels like a traditional custom home.

Evaluation of Comfort and Sound

We evaluate the interior environment of your metal home to address common challenges such as echo and temperature fluctuations. By integrating acoustical ceiling systems and sound-control drywall techniques, we reduce the 'hollow' sound often associated with large steel structures. Our design-to-completion assistance ensures that your floor plan is executed precisely as planned, from the initial framing layout to the final skim coat on the walls.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Desired R-value and type of insulation for Arizona climate efficiency.
  • Sound-control requirements for bedrooms or home offices.
  • Electrical and plumbing routing within steel-stud wall systems.
  • Matching wall textures to existing designs or personal preferences.
