Barndominium Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Barndominiums combine the durability of a metal building with the comfort of a modern home. Total Building Systems offers expert guidance and construction for metal-home projects, managing the entire process from the initial steel erection to the final interior drywall and texture.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We provide comprehensive support for barndominium projects, from supplying and erecting the steel shell to completing the residential interior. Our team handles the critical transition from a metal building to a comfortable home, including steel-stud framing, drywall, and high-end interior finishes.

Shell and Finish-Out Options

We offer flexible project scopes for barndominium builders, ranging from 'shell-only' erection to 'turnkey' interior finish-outs. During the preconstruction phase, we provide site-feasibility reviews and permit coordination to ensure your metal home meets local building codes. We also assist with preliminary budget planning to help you understand the cost differences between various insulation and finish options.

Residential Comfort and Sound Control

Sound control is a major factor in barndominiums, especially those with attached workshops. We utilize office sound-control assessments and acoustic ceiling upgrades to minimize noise transfer between living and working spaces. Our interior construction team uses steel-stud framing and professional drywall texturing to ensure the inside of your metal home feels and looks like a traditional high-end residence.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Zoning and residential permit requirements for metal-framed homes in your area.
  • Choosing between a shell-only build and a full interior finish-out.
  • Sound-control needs between the living quarters and the workshop or garage area.
  • Insulation strategies to ensure residential comfort in a steel-framed building.
