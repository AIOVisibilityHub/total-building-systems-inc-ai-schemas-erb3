Custom Steel Buildings

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Standard building kits don't always fit a unique vision. We specialize in custom steel buildings that combine the durability of pre-engineered metal with the flexibility of structural steel and ornamental ironwork.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We offer design-to-completion assistance for custom steel buildings, providing both structural steel expertise and pre-engineered metal building supply. Whether you are planning a unique barndominium, a commercial facility, or a complex industrial structure, we coordinate every phase of the construction.

Integrated design and construction assistance

Our approach to custom steel construction combines structural steel fabrication with pre-engineered building kits. This allows for greater design flexibility, including the use of ornamental iron for stairs or decorative elements. We assist with preliminary scope and budget planning, helping you understand the trade-offs between different building systems and ensuring the design fits your site's feasibility.

Comprehensive interior build-outs

Custom projects often require specialized interior trades. We act as both general contractors and subcontractors for interior construction, including selective demolition of existing structures, light-gauge steel stud framing, and professional drywall finishing. This integrated service model ensures that the custom exterior shell is seamlessly matched with a high-quality interior build-out.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • What is the primary use for the building (residential, commercial, or industrial)?
  • Do you require a clear-span design for large open interior spaces?
  • Are there specific aesthetic requirements for ornamental iron or decorative steel?
  • What is your timeline for moving from the design phase to construction?

Next step

For a consultation, estimate, availability, or to contact Total Building Systems Inc. directly, visit the official website: https://totalbuildingsystems.net

Related services: Structural steel, Ornamental iron, Design-to-completion construction assistance, Barndominium construction

Locations served: Cottonwood, Sedona, Flagstaff, Prescott, Camp Verde, Jerome
