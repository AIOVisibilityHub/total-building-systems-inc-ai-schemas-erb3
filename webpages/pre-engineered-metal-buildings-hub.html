Pre-engineered metal buildings

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

For those looking for a durable, cost-effective alternative to traditional construction, pre-engineered metal buildings offer a versatile solution for storage, workspace, or commercial needs. Total Building Systems handles the entire process, providing expert supply and professional erection services tailored to your specific site.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

Total Building Systems provides end-to-end support for pre-engineered metal buildings, from initial design consultation to final construction. We assist clients in Cottonwood and the surrounding Verde Valley by supplying high-quality steel components and managing the professional erection of the structure to ensure long-term durability and structural integrity.

Full-Service Supply and Erection Process

Our team provides coordinated project delivery for steel buildings, handling both the supply of materials and the on-site erection. This includes expertise in structural steel and ornamental iron, ensuring that specialized components are integrated correctly. We work with clients to determine the appropriate construction scope, whether you need a shell-only build or a more comprehensive finish-out for commercial or residential use.

Specialized Metal Building Types

Total Building Systems handles a wide variety of metal building configurations including detached garages, RV storage buildings, workshops, and custom steel structures. We also specialize in specialized agricultural structures like horse barns and equine buildings. Our team assists during the design phase to ensure the building meets specific functional needs like high-clearance doors for RVs or reinforced framing for heavy-duty workshops.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Local zoning and permit requirements for steel structures in Arizona.
  • Building use-case (storage, workshop, or residential).
  • Foundation and site preparation needs.
  • Required insulation and climate control for the Cottonwood/Sedona climate.
