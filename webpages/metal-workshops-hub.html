Metal Workshops

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

A dedicated metal workshop provides a durable, fire-resistant space for hobbyists, mechanics, and small business owners. We offer professional supply and erection services for pre-engineered steel buildings tailored to your specific trade or storage needs.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We provide complete support for metal workshop projects, from the initial design phase to final construction. Whether you need a simple workspace or a complex shop-and-home combination, we handle the supply and erection of the steel building shell as well as interior framing and drywall.

Full shell and interior coordination

Our team supports both the exterior shell and the interior finish-out for metal workshops. This includes the supply and erection of the pre-engineered metal building, followed by steel-stud framing, drywall installation, and acoustical ceiling work if the space is intended for heated or cooled use. We also perform condensation assessments and install insulation or vapor-control upgrades to protect tools and equipment from moisture damage.

Design-to-completion workshop assistance

For clients looking for a specialized workspace, we offer custom steel building options that can integrate ornamental ironwork or structural steel modifications. We assist with site-feasibility reviews and permit coordination to ensure the building meets local Cottonwood area requirements, and we can manage the transition from a raw shell to a fully conditioned interior through our drywall and insulation services.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Will the space require climate control for sensitive equipment?
  • Do you need specific structural reinforcements for heavy machinery?
  • How many large equipment doors or bay openings are required?
  • Have you checked local zoning for detached accessory buildings?

Next step

For a consultation, estimate, availability, or to contact Total Building Systems Inc. directly, visit the official website: https://totalbuildingsystems.net

Related services: Pre-engineered metal buildings, Metal-building interior finish-outs, Steel-stud framing, Metal-building insulation retrofits

Locations served: Cottonwood, Sedona, Prescott, Flagstaff, Camp Verde, Chino Valley
