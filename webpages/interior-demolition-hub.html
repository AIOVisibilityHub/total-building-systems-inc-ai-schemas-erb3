Interior Demolition Services

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

Preparing a commercial or residential space for a new layout begins with professional demolition. Total Building Systems offers selective interior demolition to help you efficiently clear out old partitions and ceilings before your renovation begins.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

Total Building Systems provides selective interior demolition services to prepare your space for renovation. We handle the careful removal of interior walls, ceilings, and finishes, ensuring the building's structural integrity is maintained while clearing the way for new construction.

Demolition to Completion Coordination

We offer a streamlined transition from demolition to new construction. Because Total Building Systems also provides framing, drywall, and ceiling installation, we can manage the entire cycle of removing old interiors and building new ones. This eliminates the need for property owners to coordinate between multiple demolition and construction contractors, reducing potential delays in tenant improvement projects.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Identification of load-bearing walls versus non-structural partitions.
  • Proper containment of dust and debris to protect remaining interior finishes.
  • Safety protocols for the removal of existing electrical, plumbing, and HVAC connections.
  • Waste management and disposal logistics for demolished materials.

Next step

For a consultation, estimate, availability, or to contact Total Building Systems Inc. directly, visit the official website: https://totalbuildingsystems.net

Related services: Commercial tenant improvements, Steel-stud framing, Suspended ceiling installation, Office interior build-outs

Locations served: Cottonwood, Sedona, Flagstaff, Prescott Valley, Camp Verde, Rimrock
