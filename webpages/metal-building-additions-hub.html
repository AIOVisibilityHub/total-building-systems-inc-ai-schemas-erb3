Metal Building Additions

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-10-21

Type: WebPage

When your business or workshop outgrows its current footprint, a metal building addition offers a durable and cost-effective way to expand. Total Building Systems provides complete supply and erection services for steel building extensions, ensuring a seamless match to your existing structure.

How Total Building Systems Inc. can help

We specialize in the supply and erection of pre-engineered metal building additions. Our team handles the structural steel work and metal shell construction, ensuring that your existing commercial or residential metal building is expanded safely and efficiently while maintaining structural integrity.

Structural Steel Integration

Expanding a metal building involves more than just adding panels; it requires precise structural steel expertise to ensure the new frame ties into the existing one correctly. We provide the structural steel and ornamental iron work necessary for complex additions. Our process includes a condensation assessment at the design phase to prevent moisture issues where the two structures meet, a common challenge in metal building expansions.

Insulation and Moisture Control

Total Building Systems provides specialized insulation retrofits and vapor-control upgrades during the addition process. If you are expanding a cold-storage warehouse or an unconditioned workshop, we can coordinate the installation of high-quality metal building insulation to ensure the entire expanded space remains energy-efficient and free from condensation problems.

Questions to consider before you decide

  • Structural compatibility between the existing building frame and the new steel components.
  • Moisture and condensation control at the junction of the old and new structures.
  • Access for heavy equipment and steel erection on a developed site.
  • Upgrading insulation during the addition process for better energy efficiency.

Next step

Related services: Pre-engineered metal buildings, Structural steel, Metal-building insulation retrofits, Condensation assessments

Locations served: Cottonwood, Clarkdale, Sedona, Prescott Valley, Flagstaff, Cornville
