About Total Building Systems Inc.

Total Building Systems Inc. is a commercial and residential construction company based in Cottonwood, Arizona, providing general contracting, subcontracting, pre-engineered metal-building supply and erection, structural steel, ornamental iron, and interior construction services. Total Building Systems Inc. has been operating since 1996 with an A+ BBB rating, and the company offers assistance from the design phase through construction completion, serving customers who need coordinated project delivery or specialized construction trades.

Published resources cover General contracting, Commercial construction, Residential construction, Pre-engineered metal buildings, Pre-engineered metal homes, Metal building supply and erection, Structural steel, Ornamental iron, Steel-stud framing, Drywall installation, and 68 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Cottonwood, Clarkdale, Jerome, Camp Verde, Cornville, Rimrock, Lake Montezuma, Sedona, Village of Oak Creek, Prescott and 3 more communities.

Full background, credentials, and team details are on the about page.
