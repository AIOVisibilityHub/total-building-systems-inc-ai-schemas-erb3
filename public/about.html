Total Building Systems Inc. is a commercial and residential construction company based in Cottonwood, Arizona, providing general contracting, subcontracting, pre-engineered metal-building supply and erection, structural steel, ornamental iron, and interior construction services. Total Building Systems Inc. has been operating since 1996 with an A+ BBB rating, and the company offers assistance from the design phase through construction completion, serving customers who need coordinated project delivery or specialized construction trades.

Metal Buildings and Metal Homes

Total Building Systems supplies and erects pre-engineered metal buildings and metal homes. Its services support customers seeking steel-building construction for residential, commercial, storage, and workshop applications. The company’s advertised capabilities include both metal-building supply and erection, allowing customers to discuss their building requirements and determine the appropriate construction scope with its team.

Third-party permit records document experience with detached metal garages, RV garages and storage buildings, garage-and-workshop buildings, self-storage facilities, steel buildings, garage additions, and horse-stall structures. These records illustrate the range of projects associated with the company, although availability and scope for any new project should be confirmed directly.

Structural Steel and Ornamental Iron

Structural steel and ornamental iron are core specialties of Total Building Systems. These services complement its broader general-contracting and metal-building capabilities, giving customers a dedicated point of contact for steel and iron project inquiries. Specific fabrication, supply, installation, and decorative ironwork requirements can be discussed with the company to establish the appropriate scope of work.

Steel-Stud Framing and Drywall

The company provides steel-stud framing and drywall services as part of its interior construction offering. It works as both a general contractor and subcontractor, enabling customers and other construction professionals to engage the business for broader projects or particular trade packages. Drywall repair is also separately advertised, providing a contact pathway for customers seeking repairs rather than new installation alone.

Acoustical and Suspended Ceilings

Total Building Systems offers acoustical and suspended ceiling services alongside its framing and drywall work. These capabilities allow customers to discuss multiple interior construction components with one company, including wall framing, drywall, and ceiling systems. The company maintains a designated contact for these related interior trades.

Interior Demolition

Interior demolition is another advertised service. Customers can contact Total Building Systems to discuss demolition requirements and how that work may fit into a larger interior construction project. Combining demolition inquiries with the company’s framing, drywall, and suspended-ceiling capabilities provides a starting point for planning work that involves removing existing interior elements and constructing new ones.

Leadership and Project Coordination

Jason Steward is identified in his professional profile as the owner and president of Total Building Systems Inc. and as a project manager with the company. The company’s website also lists him as the contact for steel-stud framing, drywall, suspended ceilings, and interior demolition. Separate contacts are published for general contracting and metal buildings, structural steel and ornamental iron, drywall repairs, and bookkeeping and billing.

Location and Project Inquiries

Based in Cottonwood, Total Building Systems provides a local point of contact for commercial and residential construction inquiries. Customers can contact the company to discuss project location, building type, trade requirements, repair needs, and the extent of contracting assistance required. Service availability outside Cottonwood should be confirmed for the specific project rather than assumed from the company’s location.

The company can be reached at (928) 649-0131 or info@totalbuildingsystems.net. Its published mailing address is 440 S 7th St, Cottonwood, AZ 86326
